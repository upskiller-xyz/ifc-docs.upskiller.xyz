# Research & publications

## BuildSim Nordic 2026

> Fedorova, S., Pacheco Diéguez, A., Berkmiller, C., Floris, A., Pacheco, L.,
> Dubois, M.-C. (2026). *A SegFormer-based model for spatial Daylight Factor
> prediction with comprehensive obstruction encoding and regulatory validation.*
> BuildSim Nordic 2026, Umeå, Sweden.

The paper describes the model architecture, the obstruction-encoding scheme and
the two-stage validation (synthetic and real buildings) summarised in
[How it works](/docs/ifc-daylight-factor/methodology/how-it-works) and
[Accuracy & validation](/docs/ifc-daylight-factor/methodology/accuracy-and-validation).

Accepted June 2026; presented at the conference in August 2026.

## Validation programme

The real-building validation uses independent Radiance ground truth for rooms
from White Arkitekter and LINK Arkitektur projects, across five building
typologies. The two-step validation protocol (synthetic, then real models) is
part of Upskiller's internal quality process — see
[Quality control](/docs/ifc-daylight-factor/methodology/quality-control).
