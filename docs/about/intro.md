# About

IFC Daylight Factor is built by the [upskiller](https://upskiller.xyz) team: [Stasja](https://aecincode.substack.com), [Christoph](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christoph-berkmiller-08822020a/), [Libny](https://www.linkedin.com/in/libny-pacheco-6548b95/) and [Alejandro](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alejandro-pacheco-di%C3%A9guez-06b1b238/) (BIMTech Innovations AB).

It started as [Alejandro](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alejandro-pacheco-di%C3%A9guez-06b1b238/)'s research project in [Lund University](https://www.lth.se/), continued through his work at [BAU](https://bau.se/) and [White Arkitekter](https://whitearkitekter.com/se/), and eventually led him to [IaaC](https://iaac.net)'s [MACAD](https://iaac.net/masters/master-in-advanced-computational-design-for-architecture/) program, where he crossed paths with [Libny](https://www.linkedin.com/in/libny-pacheco-6548b95/), [Christoph](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christoph-berkmiller-08822020a/), and [Stasja](https://aecincode.substack.com).

After graduating, the four of them decided to turn Alejandro's long-standing daylight idea into a real platform. IFC Daylight Factor was built from scratch, drawing on years of prior research and the team's combined expertise in architecture, machine learning, and software development.

This open, browser-based IFC Daylight Factor tool was developed with support from [Belysningsstiftelsen](https://belysningsstiftelsen.se/). [Marie-Claude Dubois](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marie-claude-dubois-7a696736/) at [LTH](https://www.lth.se/) contributed to the scientific validation of our approach. A more advanced ML model and a Revit commercial plugin was built independently with support from [Vinnova](https://www.vinnova.se/), [Almi](https://www.almi.se/), and [Scaleway](https://www.scaleway.com/) and in collaboration with [Alberto Floris](https://github.com/sirolf-otrebla).
